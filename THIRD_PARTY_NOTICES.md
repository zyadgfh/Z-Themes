- https://github.com/MyCodeIsntWorking/HUAWEI_Theme_Elegance-Advanced

## Independence

Z Aurora is an unofficial iOS-inspired theme. It is not an Apple design-system distribution and is not affiliated with Apple, Huawei or Framework7. Apple artwork, SF Symbols, SF Pro fonts, Apple logos and stock iOS wallpapers are not included. Third-party applications use generic themed symbols, not their official brand artwork. Wallpaper and icon tile backgrounds are original programmatically generated artwork.
